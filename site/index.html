Cabinet type
Face frame: a wood frame narrower than the box around the opening. Frameless: the opening is the whole box.

Your door Why mount it?

The door comes off its hinges and becomes the pull-out's front, so nothing blocks the sides. A door that only opens to 90° blocks part of the hinge side. 170° hinges help.
