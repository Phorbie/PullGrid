Parameters

Every input the generator and scad/pullgrid.scad accept. All dimensions are in mm inside the model; the website converts inches for you. -1 means auto.

PullGrid models a cabinet as a shape with obstacles in it: an opening in the front plane, an interior that can taper toward the back, fixed shelves that split it into tiers, and a list of keep-out boxes. Hinges are keep-outs generated for you (and dropped entirely when the door is mounted).

Coordinates

Relative to the cabinet interior: x across from the left wall, y depth from the front plane (the back of the face frame; the front edge of a frameless box), z up from the floor.

Basic

Param Meaning
opening_w, opening_h Clear opening you can pass something through
interior_depth Front plane to back wall, at the floor
frame_type face_frame or frameless. Drives the offset defaults
hinge_side left, right, none. Only used when the door is kept hinged
shelves Fixed shelves: [top, top, ...], each shelf's top surface above the interior floor, in any order. Each gap gets its own pull-out (tier). Empty = none

Door

Param Default Meaning
door_mode mount mount: the door comes off its hinges and screws to printed brackets on the front plate. keep: the door stays hinged; hinges become keep-outs. none: no door; nothing to dodge. With keep or none the front plate is solid and carries the front handles
door_thickness 19 Door mount needs at least 15
door_overlay 12.7 How far the door overlaps the opening per side. Preview only
face_frame_thickness -1 Face frame depth, front to back. Auto: 19 face frame, 0 frameless. Sets the bracket depth
door_opens past_90 Keep mode: only_90 if something stops the door at 90°. Then the pull-out next to the hinges is one-sided (see sides)

With a mounted door and more than one tier, every tier is fixed to the door: they share the shortest slide length and the deepest front plane, and get one row of brackets each instead of two.

Front handles

Only when the front plate is the face (door_mode = keep or none). A mounted door keeps its own pull.

Param Default Meaning
handle slot slot: finger slots cut through the plate. bar, arch, knob: printed, screwed on from inside the plate (one screw per post). pull: a store-bought cabinet pull, bolted on with short machine screws. none
handle_dir horizontal vertical turns slots, bars, arches and pulls upright
handle_pos top top, middle or bottom of the front
handle_across center With one handle: left, center or right
handle_count -1 Per pull-out. Auto: one in the middle, or one each side when a board runs down the middle (its groove is in the way). 2 = side by side; vertical ones go near each edge
handle_size -1 Slot length, screw spacing (bar, arch, pull) or knob diameter. Auto: 90 slot, 96 bar/arch/pull, 32 knob. Pulls come in 96 mm (3¾″) and 128 mm (5″) everywhere

Anything that goes through the plate stays clear of the board grooves, the plate seams and their keys. A horizontal slot across a groove gets a bridge over it. If a handle has to move to fit, the report says so. Printed handles stick out 34 mm (knobs 32, pulls about 35); with a kept door the pull-out sits back far enough for the door to close over them.

Advanced: offsets and odd shapes

Param Auto value Meaning
offset_left/right 10 face frame, 0 frameless Face frame overhang past the interior wall
offset_bottom 15 / 0 Bottom rail height above the floor. The moving parts must clear it
offset_top 10 / 0 Top rail drop
rear_w, rear_h same as front Non-square cabinets; rear assumed centered
hinges 2–4 Euro hinges by door height, 80 mm from the ends, 12 mm (face frame) / 20 mm intrusion [[center_z, intrusion, height, depth], ...]. Keep mode only
door_intrusion 0 Keep mode: how far a door at 90° sticks into the opening (inset doors). Overlay doors: 0
shelf_thickness 19 Fixed shelves
shelf_setback 0 Fixed shelves: front edge behind the front plane. Tiers on shelves start this far back

The auto values are educated guesses. They need checking against real cabinets.

Expert

Param Meaning
keepouts [[x, y, z, w, d, h], ...]: plumbing, disposal, shelf pin rows

How keep-outs are solved

A pull-out sweeps its whole profile through every depth as it slides out, so anything whose x–z footprint overlaps the frame blocks it, wherever it sits in depth. The one escape is ending the frame in front of it.

For each keep-out the solver tries five dodges (shrink from the left, right, bottom or top, or shorten from the back) and keeps the one that loses the least volume. It's greedy and order-dependent: good enough for hinges plus a pipe.

The floor rails stand below the frame, so a keep-out blocks anything from the tier's floor up. Raising the floor is only allowed when the keep-out covers the tier's whole width and rail run: the rails then stand on top of it. Anything smaller gets one of the other dodges.

Each tier is solved separately, in the slice between its shelf (or the floor) and the shelf above (or the opening top), so a keep-out only costs the tiers it reaches.

Pull-out

Param Default Meaning
panel_count 1 openGrid panels per pull-out, running front to back
panel_thickness 6.8 6.8 = openGrid Full (double-sided), 4.0 = Lite
pullouts -1 (auto) Side-by-side pull-outs. Auto = as few as fit the print bed
bed_size 256 Parts are split to fit, with a 6 mm margin
reach_from auto both, left or right for every pull-out at once. One side = a single panel against the other side, all accessories facing you (panel_count is ignored)
sides [] Per pull-out, beating reach_from: [[tier, position, "both" | "left" | "right" | "auto"], ...], position counted from the left. Auto: one-sided next to the hinges of a kept door with door_opens = only_90; side-by-side pull-outs on a mounted door face outward; otherwise both

The frame is sized to whole 28 mm openGrid cells, so it can come out up to one cell shorter and shallower than the space allows.

Hardware

Param Default Meaning
region inch inch (US, Canada) or metric (UK, IE, AU, NZ). Sets the slide length list (10–28″ in 2″ steps, or 250–600 mm in 50 mm steps), angle sizes, and shopping-list names. The website picks it from your browser's language
rail_type angle angle: aluminum angle floor rails. printed: printed rails, no metalwork
angle_thickness -1 Angle wall thickness. Auto: 3.2 inch, 3.0 metric
slide_t 13.0 Slide clearance per side. The spec is ½″ (12.7) plus up to 1/32″, so 13.0 aims mid-range
slide_h 45 Slide body height

The angle must reach above the slide's screw line: leg ≥ slide bottom + 28.5 mm. The generator picks 1½″ / 40 mm, or 2″ / 50 mm when the bottom rail is tall, and warns if neither reaches. With several tiers it uses the bigger size everywhere, so you buy one stock size.

Clearances

Param Default Meaning
clear_side 3 Each side, inside the opening
clear_top 5 Above the frame
clear_rear 10 Behind the rear plate. Keep ≥ 10 for the tie-rod nuts
clear_front -1 In front of the front plate. Auto: max(0, 9 − face frame depth), which leaves room for the tie-rod nuts and door brackets; with a kept door and handles that stick out, enough for the door to close over them
